The Impact of Initial Public Offerings on Rival 
Firms: Evidence from KOSDAQ Market
Ming-Hui Kang*, Yoon-Soo Lim**
Abstract   
  Our paper extends previous studies through a comprehensive examination of the 
impact of IPOs on the rivals. This study is guided by three primary motives: first, to 
investigate whether the impact of IPOs on the rivals exist, and second, to examine 
whether rivals’ reactions in response to venture-backed IPOs are different from that 
of non venture-backed IPOs. Third, we also attempt to test whether the rivals react 
differently in response to IPOs according to the difference in specific characteristics. 
Results in our study show that IPOs have competitive effects on rivals. Our research 
also shows that venture-backed IPOs have significantly negative impacts on rivals, but 
rivals have insignificant reactions to non venture-backed IPOs. 
Keywords : IPOs(initial public offerings), Venture Capital, Herfindahl Index
JEL Classification : G3, G14

Ⅰ. Introduction
As a classic and hot issue in finance field, initial public offerings (IPOs) have 
attracted many researchers’ attentions. In recent years, a large number of related 
studies have been done mainly focusing on two aspects: the underpricing of IPOs 
and subsequent long-run underperformance. However, relatively little evidence has 
been found to explain the impact of IPOs on corresponding rivals in the same 
industry. Thus, present research is conducted to fill the research gap.
When a company plans to go public, the news will be released to the market 
rapidly, and then investors may interpret valuable information revealed by an IPO 
as a signal of the future prospe ct of the  firm  or its corre sponding industry. 
Therefore, it’s reasonable to estimate that IPO announcements are likely to exert 
effects on rival firms within the same industry. However, previ ous studies 
regarding the impact of IPOs on corresponding rivals in the same industry have 
not achieved a consistent conclusion. Some scholars argue that a firm’s decisions 
to go public may have competitive effects on corresponding rivals. It is because 
that the IPOs increase the visibility of the firm and raise funds which can be 
used to expand and compete more effectively, thus the newly public firms can 
gain m ore the m arket shares. T hese advantages for IPO s m ay exert negative 
effects on rivals. On the contrary, some scholars suggest that IPO announcements

Ⅴ.Conclusion
As mentioned above, the first motiveof our study is to investigate whether the 
intra-industry valuation effects of IPOs on the corresponding rivals exist. Results 
in our study show that rivals have significantly negative reactions in response to 
IPO announcements. This finding implies that IPO firms contribute the competitive 
threat for rivals in the same industry, since the IPO firms not only raise funds 
that the firms can use to expand and compete more effectively, but also the IPO 
firm obtain higher market shares from its private competitors.
The second research topic in our study is to examine whether venture-backed

venture-backed IPOs. Previous empirical findings suggest that venture- backed 
IPO firms signal superior information to the market when compar ed to non 
venture-backed IPO firms, which means that venture-backed IPOs should have 
different influences on corresponding rivals when compared to non venture-back 
IPOs. Our empirical findings generally support the hypothesis. By partitioning the 
sample into venture-backed IPOs and non venture-backed IPOs, ou r research 
shows that a venture-backed IPO have significantly negative imp act on 
corresponding rivals in the same industry, but corresponding ri vals have 
insignificant reaction to a non venture-backed IPO. This finding suggests that the 
presence of venture capitalists have constituted competitive th reats for 
corresponding rivals in the same industry.

several variables to examine the direction and magnitude of rivals’ reactions in

IPO이  경쟁기업에 미치는 영향에
관한 연구- 코스닥 시장을 중심으로
강 명 혜*․ 임 윤 수**
요  약
  본 연구는 IPO이 동종 산업 내에서 경쟁기업에 대한 효과에 대한 기존 선행연구들
을  광범위하게 확장하려고 시도하였다. 본 연구의 목적은 다음과 같다. 첫째, IPO 이
후에 경쟁기업간에 교차하여 나타날 수 있는 영향에 대해 분석하였다. 둘째, 벤처캐피
탈에 투자된 IPO가 벤처캐피탈에 투자되지 않는 IPO보다 산업내 경쟁기업에 더 많은 
영향을 주는지에 대해 분석하였다. 본 연구의 주요 연구결과는 다음과 같다. 첫째, 
IPO는 경쟁기업의 가치에 경쟁적인 영향을 줄 수 있는 것으로 나타났다. 벤처캐피탈
에 투자된 IPO기업가 경쟁기업에 유의적인 부(-)의 영향을 미칠 수 있다는 결과가 나
타난 것에 비해, 벤처캐피탈에 투자되지 않는 IPO기업의 경우는 유의하지 않은 결과
가 나타났다.
